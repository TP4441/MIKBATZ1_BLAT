# Surface Brief: index.html (מבחני עבר – מקבץ 1)

- **Scope:** Full single-page study workbench (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`) across Practice, Exam Simulation, Mistake Drill, Flashcards, and Analytics.
- **Visitor Mode:** Operate

## Direction contract

THESIS: Replace the bloated, low-contrast SaaS quiz layout with a calm, high-legibility academic study edition ("Technion Monograph") where the active clinical question leads from pixel one on both desktop and mobile, and filtering stays tucked into a single expandable index strip.

OWN-WORLD: Warm archival paper ground (`#F9F7F2`), crisp white reading plates (`#FFFFFF`) with warm hairline rules (`#DFDBD0`), deep Haifa cobalt shell and primary actions (`#163A5F`), muted parchment secondary surfaces (`#F2EFE6`), and WCAG AA+ semantic clinical states (`#15803D` success, `#B91C1C` danger, `#92400E` on `#FEF3C7` warning/appeal), paired with a low-glare "Night Desk" dark theme (`#0B121B` / `#131E2C`), `Heebo` bilingual RTL/LTR typography (`65–72ch` stem measure, `tabular-nums`), and unified 1.75px-stroke SVG iconography.

STORY: A Technion medical student opens the link on a laptop at the library or on a phone from bed, sees their active question immediately without scrolling past filter walls, solves with rapid keyboard keys or thumb-friendly mobile dock controls, inspects zoomable clinical figures, checks spoiler-safe peer pearls, and tracks subject/year readiness in one cohesive environment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A single slim cobalt header (`#163A5F`) holds brand title, 5 mode tabs, anonymous device alias, and theme/backup/export utilities; directly below sits a 1-line collapsed Exam Index Strip (`search` + active filter summary + question counter + `הרחב סינון` toggle), placing the full Question Card (badges, stem, clinical figure beside stem on desktop when present, 5 options with inline strike-out, and navigation—anchored as a sticky thumb dock on mobile `<=720px`) cleanly above the fold.

FORM: Technion Monograph (Candidate 1, user-selected `IMPECCABLE'S PICK` from direction roll, `seed:a67b90681a4493ba`), built code-first for Operate mode.
